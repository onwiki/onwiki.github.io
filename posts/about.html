About this Journal

A brief description of the why and who behind this

A pic taken during a storm, right before I wrote my first journal entry

I figure that if you're actually here looking through this journal then I may as well right a little something explaining it.  

Firstly, the word 'wiki' comes from the Hawaiian phrase wikiwiki, which means "quick" - a meaning I did not name this site after. Wiki has since been used in websites like Wiki-pedia and Wiki-How to describe a collection of knowledge shaped by many people. The reason why the name of this here site features this word is for two main reasons: 

1 ) At the time of writing this, I'm hosting this site using a website called Github, and onwiki was the shortest username available that had some relevance so my goal of an internet journal. 

2 ) The more I thought of the name and the word wiki the more it made sense. We, as individuals, are a collection of knowledge and experiences influenced from the people in our lives, much like a Wiki website is a collection of knowledge or opinions from many people. While this site may not be a Wiki in the traditional sense, I think the person writing it (me) could be compared to one, as we all could be. Maybe I'm just post-justifying the name...

I like to write, though whether or not I am any good is an entirely different thing. There is a quote by Peter De Vreis (which is often wrongly credited as belonging to Hemingway) that is "write drunk, edit sober". I'm not one to get drunk often but I feel like the same logic applies to when someone is overcome - or drunk - with emotion of any kind. I like to write when I am feeling really strongly in any way, then look. through what I wrote at a future date when I'm more level headed. I like to write about anything, meaningful or meaningless, and I like reading what others on the internet write so I thought I would start my own blog-esque page. I put my email on the bottom of every page so that if anyone ever reads a post or wants to know more about a project or anything then they would be able to send me an email. I usually reply pretty fast. 

I also added a Collage page to this site (it's the button on the home page with the four squares in it) and am trying to full it up with photos as a way to try cut back on the desire to post on social media but still maintain an outlet for any pics. Like my writing, that's not to say any of the pictures will be good but it'll at least keep me sane for a little bit.

I like the rain because it feels like it's 'cleansing' the ground with it's showers, whilst also acting as a large blanket keeping all of us people underneath it connected and together in some way. Sometimes I feel like memory lane can be a painful place to visit because of all the connections to people and places we no longer have in our lives that we once did. People - or more accurately a version of them - that now only exist in our minds. I think it's wise to not dwell on the past though, as memory lane is place best visited, but not lived. The future is uncertain but at least that means it can be influenced, and that's what gives it more meaning than the past.
