About this Journal

Published on 2026-08-05 at 19:40 UTC+12 by Oscar N.
If you're tuning into my journal here with ONWiki, I figure I may as well write a bit of an "about" page for anyone curious enough to read it. 

The word 'wiki' comes from the Hawaiian phrase wikiwiki, which means "quick", a meaning I did not name this site after. Wiki has since been used in websites like Wiki-pedia and Wiki-How to describe a collection of knowledge shaped by many people. The reason why the name of this here site (ONWiki) features this word is for two main reasons. 

1 ) being that at the time of writing this, I'm hosting this website using Github and onwiki was the shortest, available username I could select that had some relevance so my goal of having an internet journal, and 

2 ) the more I thought of the name the more it made sense. We're all our own little Wiki of ourself. We're a personal collection of knowledge and experiences, and everything we create is built from the contributions, ideas, and influences of many people. When I think of a Wiki, that is what comes to mind. 

ONWiki may be my solo site encapsulating my thoughts (I think that's the right way to use encapsulating...) but the person behind it (me) represents the idea of a Wiki enough that I think the name is justified. Maybe I'm just post-justifying the name...

I like to write, anything meaningful or meaningless, and I like reading what others on the internet write so I thought I would start my own blog-esque page. I put my email on the bottom of every page so that if anyone ever reads a post or wants to know more about a project or anything, they can send me an email. I usually reply pretty fast.
